# PS-449 - Immutable finalized billing with current-period adjustments

## Canonical owner

- `src/services/billing-finalization-policy.ts` owns close admission, the
  transaction boundary, client advisory locking, frozen invoice totals,
  immutable finalization summaries, signed adjustment idempotency, regeneration
  reconciliation, and remaining-balance enforcement.
- `src/services/billing-invoice-totals.ts` remains the shared backend totals
  owner used by invoice reads and finalization. Routes and React do not
  recompute money.
- `drizzle/0065_billing_close_workflow.sql` and
  `drizzle/0074_billing_current_period_adjustments.sql` are the database
  backstops. They
  serialize overlapping client-period writes, block mutations inside closed
  periods, keep finalizations and adjustments append-only, require a one-to-one
  current-period billing-line projection, and prevent over-crediting.
- `src/routes/billing.ts` is thin transport: permission, tenant scope,
  authenticated actor, policy delegation, response, and audit event.
- `BillingView.tsx` and `BillingCloseWorkflowPanel.tsx` display backend totals
  and send operator intent only.

The earliest imperfect-data boundary is an underlying order or money sidecar
changing after its invoice is finalized. Generation recalculates those orders
through the same canonical calculator, leaves every finalized line unchanged,
and asks the policy owner to append only the remaining signed delta in the
backend-selected current billing day. The migration and service use the same
per-client transaction advisory lock, so finalize, regeneration, and manual
adjustment posting cannot interleave around the close fact.

## Implementation history

The original close workflow remains the frozen source of truth. PS-449 extends
it with one append-only correction path:

- finalized source lines are never deleted, rebuilt, or updated;
- regeneration reports adjusted and untouched finalized-order counts;
- decreases create credits and increases create debits in the current period;
- repeated regeneration is idempotent by recomputing the remaining delta under
  the client advisory lock;
- summaries, details, HTML, XLSX, CSV, and React consume the signed backend row;
- the finalize button and adjustment form send intent only.

the real 0059/0065/0071/0074 migrations. Certification does not finalize or
adjust a live invoice, connect to a live provider, buy postage, create or print
labels, notify a marketplace, or mutate production billing, order, shipment,
or inventory rows.

The migrated behavior suite proves the prior legacy correction write fails
closed instead of creating an unprojected adjustment; operators must keep
correction creation disabled until the current-period writer is restored.
