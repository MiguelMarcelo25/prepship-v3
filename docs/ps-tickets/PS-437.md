# PS-437 — Unified customer shipping money

## Outcome

PrepShip is now the canonical owner of customer shipping money for outbound and
return shipments. Client Portal persists the exact purchased label cost, asks
PrepShip to freeze the policy result, and consumes that frozen result without
recomputing billing truth.

Runtime implementation is deployed from:

- PrepShip `prepshipv4-stable`: `7c8cca4d1312379257948b728257be5353b60d90`
- Client Portal `main`: `fb3cfb34d31138e2aabaa5a326d111787eb0f105`
  (implementation `b3a1dc83`, fixture correction `59eb5ac3`, final guard
  correction `fb3cfb34`)

## Root cause and placement

- **Business rule:** purchased carrier cost, customer shipping charge, shipping
  margin, provenance, and policy version must be one immutable tuple.
- **Canonical owner:**
  `src/services/customer-shipping-money.ts` and
  `src/services/customer-shipping-money-snapshot.ts` in PrepShip.
- **Imperfect-data entry point:** Client Portal return label persistence could
  retain provider cost components without populating `selected_rate_cost`, while
  local Client Portal helpers independently derived a customer rate.
- **Unsafe duplicate owners removed:** Client Portal no longer owns the customer
  shipping formula or independently chooses the authoritative return rate.
- **Caller delegation:** PrepShip Billing and return read models use the canonical
  owner/frozen tuple; Client Portal calls the scoped PrepShip freeze endpoint and
  renders/aggregates the returned persisted value.
- **Frontend role:** display and action only. No authoritative cost, markup,
  customer charge, or margin policy was moved into React.

## PrepShip changes

1. Added a canonical customer-shipping-money decision service that resolves the
   purchased cost through existing Billing policy, including the HUGRAB override.
2. Added strict frozen-tuple parsing with policy version `ps-437-v1` for:
   `selectedRateCost`, `cShippingRateAmount`, `shippingMarginAmount`,
   `shippingMarginPct`, `customerRateSource`, and `rateCostSource`.
3. Added the client-scoped internal freeze endpoint. It rejects missing exact
   selected cost, inactive/voided/non-return shipments, unauthorized client
   scope, and partial legacy objects.
4. Made Billing and return/order read models consume the same backend-owned
   tuple instead of independently deriving customer shipping money.
5. Kept route/UI adapters thin: backend DTOs expose the frozen display fact and
   the web client passes it through.
6. Added PS-437 guards for policy boundaries, client scope, fail-closed legacy
   input, frozen tuple completeness, and non-disclosure of internal cost/margin
   fields from the freeze response.

## Client Portal changes

1. Persisted the exact provider-selected return-label cost at the label receipt
   boundary before requesting a freeze.
2. Added a narrow PrepShip client that delegates the freeze operation to the
   canonical backend owner.
3. Retired the duplicate Client Portal customer-shipping-rate service and local
   formula branches.
4. Updated return, shipment, Billing summary, and DTO consumers to use the
   policy-versioned frozen amount and fail closed when authoritative money is
   unavailable.
5. Updated integration fixtures and the retirement guard so the CI suite proves
   the new cross-service boundary.

## Production historical reconciliation

The production read-only audit found five historical return shipments:

- Three `test_offline` fixtures had an explicit zero cost/rate. They remain
  unchanged and are classified as intentional no-charge test records.
- Two real `prepship_return_v2` records had exact persisted provider-label
  receipts but were missing `selected_rate_cost` and the frozen policy tuple.

Before applying, the operator tool proved all of the following and rolled its
dry-run transaction back:

- exactly two fixed candidates matched their return/shipment IDs and references;
- provider label key and creation time existed;
- `cost`, `label_cost`, and the selected-rate envelope agreed exactly;
- neither shipment was voided;
- no return Billing line, invoice, or finalized Billing period existed;
- the canonical PrepShip policy resolved both customer amounts to `$6.77`.

The approved bounded repair then produced:

| Shipment | Selected cost | Customer rate | Margin | Margin % |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 28887 | $5.58 | $6.77 | $1.19 | 17.6% |
| 28889 | $5.70 | $6.77 | $1.07 | 15.8% |

The post-apply read-only audit reports both rows as `frozen_policy_tuple`, policy
version `ps-437-v1`, source `hugrab_shipping_rate_override`, and delta `$0.00`
from the canonical decision. Durable machine-readable evidence is in
`docs/final-review/evidence/PS-437-return-reconciliation.json`.

## Shipped-data override and safety

The reconciliation was performed under the user's exact override
`unlock shipped data` and separate approval to proceed. Protected data touched:

- production `shipments` rows `28887` and `28889`: only
  `selected_rate_cost`, the policy tuple merged into `selected_rate_json`, and
  `updated_at`;
- production `returns` rows `4` and `6`: only the compatibility alias
  `return_customer_shipping_rate` and `updated_at`.

Protections were not weakened. No shipped/cancelled status, order row, tracking
number, label URL, provider key, package/inventory fact, existing cost component,
Billing line, invoice, or finalization was changed. The operation made zero
carrier/provider requests, label purchases/voids, postage charges, marketplace
notifications, print jobs, or inventory movements.
