## The symptom this ticket exists to close

The operator UI shows a customer shipping rate (e.g. `$9.04` on order `200015261150723`). The Client Portal shows **`Pending`** for the same order.

Same database, same `shipments` row. The operator view **recomputes** live from `selected_rate_json` plus billing policy; the portal reads **only** the version-gated frozen tuple and has nothing to show. It is a coverage gap, not a disagreement about the amount. `Ext. Label` rows correctly show no rate on either side.

**PS-508 is not done until that `Pending` is gone for ordinary outbound shipments.** No amount of writer correctness substitutes for that.

## Complete

### Staged purchase-path writer — PASS 94%
Freeze at `persistCreatedLabel` (the canonical outbound producer, 2 code-level call sites), inside the committed ship transaction. Includes, after two BLOCK rulings:

- **Purchased-provider gate.** House money follows the PURCHASE, not the stamp. `captureRealizedHouseMargin` is gated on `directProviderKey === 'shipp'`; deriving from `best_rate_json` + opt-in alone was a *different* eligibility boundary, and a stale SHIPP stamp plus a non-SHIPP purchase froze house money where billing applies carrier markup (~$1.00/shipment in the reproduction).
- **SAVEPOINT, not a bare try/catch.** A failed PostgreSQL statement aborts the whole transaction; catching the JS exception does not restore it. Proven by mutation: removing the savepoint yields `25P02`.
- **House rate derived in-transaction**, because `order_competitive_rate` does not exist at label-commit — `captureRealizedHouseMargin` runs `timer.background` *after* the ship transaction commits and keys on a `shipment_id` that does not exist until the INSERT returns.
- Two coexisting policy versions (`ps-437-v1`, `ps-508-v1`), house provenance as a distinct third `customerRateSource`, and `billingDescriptionSuffix` as an eighth field (billing consumes it, and description participates in the duplicate-suppression unique index).

### Classifier, audit and watermark infrastructure
- **Five-state classifier** — `valid_ps508` / `valid_ps437` / `legacy_absent` / `malformed_known_version` / `unknown_version`. Safety property: **only `legacy_absent` may take the recompute fallback.**
- **Read-only coverage + shadow audit**, run as a Render one-off job (not a workstation), session pinned `READ ONLY` at the server, operator from authenticated `github.actor`, no apply mode, report transcribed into the Actions log.
- **Cutover watermark split**, with an activation blocker when a post-watermark row lacks a tuple.
- **Deterministic per-source timeline** — `count, min/max(create_date), min/max(id)`, no row limit.

### Malformed-version handling — accepted
`freezeOutboundCustomerShippingMoney` returns a discriminated `OutboundFreezeOutcome` instead of `Frozen | null`. A malformed tuple used to return `null` — byte-identical to "skipped, not applicable" — so a row written WRONG was indistinguishable from one correctly left alone, and billing would have recomputed it as ordinary legacy history. Neither `malformed_known_version` nor `unknown_version` is ever rewritten: the first is evidence of a writer defect, the second may belong to a newer policy. `OutboundFreezeSkipReason` is a closed union of nine values.

| `shipstation_fulfillment` | 2 | 2026-05-27 | 2026-05-29 | — |

**Purchase-path ingress stopped between 2026-07-07 and 2026-07-24. Only ShipStation sync is still producing rows.**

Two caveats that must travel with this table:

1. **`min(id)`/`max(id)` are computed independently of `min/max(create_date)`** — they are not the IDs belonging to those dates.
2. **This proves ShipStation is 100% of recent row *ingress*. It does NOT prove 100% of those rows should be *billed*.** That depends on the unanswered billability decision.

An earlier claim that purchasing stopped on 2026-08-10 was **wrong**: it was inferred from ID ordering, and the newest-500 sample only reached back to Aug 10, so the boundary of the sample was reported as the cessation date.

## What remains, in mandated order

Each step must be safe if the next never happens.

### 1. PS-509 architecture/evidence packet — **the single next step**

No code. The packet is complete only when it freezes:

1. product-owner billability policy;
2. the canonical sync writer and all callers;
3. eligible and excluded populations, with production counts;
4. honest snapshot timing — purchase-time only when proved, otherwise explicitly named **sync-ingestion-time** provenance;
5. authoritative purchased-provider and house eligibility;
6. first-ingestion, replay, partial payload, race, correction and void rules;
7. transaction and idempotency boundaries;
8. malformed/unknown snapshot handling;
9. end-to-end acceptance evidence, sync ingestion → Billing shadow → Portal projection.

**May proceed now, read-only:** the code trace `ShipStation payload → normalization → canonical insert/update owner → first authoritative cost → replay/correction/void → transaction commit`, and population classification counts.

**Blocked on the product owner:** which synced shipments are intended to receive a customer shipping charge, distinguishing (a) positive authoritative carrier-cost billable shipments, (b) genuine external labels with no customer-rate evidence, (c) provisional/incomplete receipts, (d) voided or corrected labels, (e) returns/replacements/tests. **Source name alone cannot decide billability.**

### 2. PS-509 implementation
Only after the packet is accepted.

### 3. Billing shadow + tuple precedence
Deployed **disabled**, run in shadow through complete invoice generation including finalized-order reconciliation. Owner decision on record: **bill the tuple, log divergence** — but "log" must mean durable structured records with thresholds and a circuit breaker, not a console warning, or a systematic error accumulates across an invoice period. Billing must consume the tuple **atomically** — amount *and* `billingDescriptionSuffix` *and* provenance *and* policy version — or line money and line identity come from different policies.

### 4. Evidence-qualified historical handling
**Not a blanket backfill.** Recomputing old customer money from present-day policy manufactures a label-time fact that may never have been true. Partition: exact contemporaneous evidence (may be repaired); an existing generated/finalized billing line (preserve as financial history); no contemporaneous evidence (do not backfill — retain explicit legacy recompute or `Pending`). Must refuse returns, replacements, voids, external labels, tests, bundle children and anything already invoiced.

### 5. Billing activation
**Billing leads Portal.** Portal-first would expose tuple money to customers while invoices still come from recomputation — two independently activated authorities.

### 6. Portal acceptance
Four sites pin the `ps-437-v1` literal (TS reader, SQL gate, HTTP-boundary validator, audit script); none import the constant. Returns and replacements must remain valid under `ps-437-v1` with regression proof.

### 7. Observation cycle
At least one complete billing generation/finalization cycle, with an operator-reviewed reconciliation queue and a flag-off rollback path.

### 8. Fail-closed — split three ways
Writer fail-closed for new labels; Billing fail-closed for post-watermark missing/invalid snapshots; **explicit legacy fallback for pre-watermark absence.** A global "missing tuple = fail" rule would strand historical rows that can never receive an honest snapshot.

Guards designed to fail at cutover and requiring faithful re-anchoring: `ps-437-customer-shipping-money-guard.ts:127`, plus the two PS-508 checks asserting billing still recomputes and nothing opts into v2.

## Point of no return

**The first invoice finalized under tuple precedence.** Before that, everything is a flag flip. After it, rollback requires credit/debit notes against immutable financial history.

Evidence required before it: exact-SHA coverage manifest; counts by snapshot class; tuple-vs-recompute delta with per-client and per-source grouping; an approved disposition for every non-zero divergence; **zero malformed/unknown snapshots** in the activation population; a full Billing generation dry run including finalized reconciliation, proving no unintended credits or debits; behavioural tests for amount and suffix including HUGRAB and house paths; return/replacement regression proof; Portal parity proof; deployed PrepShip and Portal SHAs; named operator approval and a documented rollback procedure.

## Known gaps

- The staged writer has **no post-watermark production execution** — its live behaviour is unproven.
- Concurrent double-purchase behaviour is unproven; PGlite is single-backend.
- The eligibility gate is pinned structurally, not driven through `persistCreatedLabel` end to end.
- `args.tx ?? db` remains permissive at `labels.ts` — must be tightened before the fail-closed step.
- `scripts/` is **not typechecked** (`tsconfig.json` includes only `src/**`), so guard scripts have no type coverage.
- This repo's CI uses `cancel-in-progress: false`: pushes queue rather than supersede, and a run-level `cancelled` can hide a passing guard-pack job. **Read CI at job level.**
